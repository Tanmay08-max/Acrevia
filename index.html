A few things to know

Questions before you start?

When is Acrevia launching?

The planned launch is 1 January 2027. The early-access form in this wireframe previews how visitors could register interest; it does not submit or store enquiries.

Is this for residential or commercial real estate?

Both. The proposed experience covers residential sales and rentals, as well as commercial sales and leasing. Final workflows and feature entitlements will be confirmed before launch.

What does the free plan include?

Basic is free for up to two users and excludes AI agents. Record limits, storage and the detailed feature list have not yet been specified.

How does Standard pricing work?

Standard costs ₹999 per user per month. Five Standard users have a base subscription cost of ₹4,995 per month. Tax treatment and any additional charges are yet to be confirmed.

Are AI agents included in Standard or Business?

Paid-plan AI entitlements, supported agents, usage allowances and any additional fees are still to be confirmed. Basic does not include AI agents.

How do I choose the Business plan?

Use Contact us to discuss your team size and requirements. Business pricing will be provided after that discussion.
